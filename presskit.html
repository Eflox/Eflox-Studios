Description

A 2D tactical shooter where nobody sees the whole map.

Short — 298 characters

A competitive 2D tactical shooter with fluid parkour movement and fog of war vision. Battle in 5v5 matches across multiple game modes including bomb defusal, team deathmatch, and free-for-all. Manage a round-based economy, coordinate tactics with your team, and climb the competitive ranked ladder.

Long — main description

BREACH: Zero Hour is a competitive 2D tactical shooter with fluid parkour movement and fog of war vision. Work together as a team in 5v5 matches across multiple game modes, including bomb defusal, team deathmatch and free-for-all.

Nobody sees the whole map. Vault, slide and wall-run through pixel-art callouts, call out what you see, and plant the bomb before the enemy hears you coming.

Manage a round-based economy, coordinate tactics with your team, and climb the competitive ranked ladder.
